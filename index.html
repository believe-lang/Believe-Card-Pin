How long does delivery take?
Once your payment is confirmed, your scratch card details (serial number + PIN) are sent to you on WhatsApp. Most orders are completed within 5 minutes. During very busy periods it may take up to 30 minutes.
How do I pay?
After you send your order on WhatsApp, we reply with our bank account details. Make a transfer for the exact amount and send us a screenshot or the transaction reference. Once confirmed, we deliver your PIN immediately.
Are these cards genuine?
Yes. Every card we sell is an original, unused scratch card PIN. We verify each one before sending it out. If a PIN fails to work, contact us immediately and we will replace it or refund you.
Can one card be used for multiple results?
No. Each WAEC or NECO scratch card is valid for one result check only. If you need to check more than one result, you'll need a separate card for each.
What if I have a problem after buying?
Just message us on WhatsApp. We'll guide you through checking your result step by step, and if the card itself has an issue we'll sort it out.
Do you sell in bulk?
Yes. If you're a school, café or agent buying in bulk, message us on WhatsApp for a special discounted rate.
